email-header-check

Who really sent this email?

Paste an email's raw headers to see who actually sent it, whether it passed SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and the route it took to reach you. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you paste is uploaded anywhere.

How to get the headers
  • Gmail: open the email → ⋮ menu → Show original → Copy to clipboard.
  • Outlook on the web: open the email → ⋯ menu → View → View message source.
  • Outlook desktop: open the email → File → Properties → copy Internet headers.
  • Apple Mail: select the email → View → Message → All Headers.
  • Yahoo Mail: open the email → ⋯ menu → View raw message.

Pasting the whole message is fine too; everything after the headers is ignored.

The examples are made up.
